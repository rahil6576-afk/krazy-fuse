# Office Escape — Visual Upgrade v2

This version keeps the original gameplay systems and focuses on a stronger visual identity:

- Cool, muted architectural office background with layered glass, skyline, desks and perspective floor.
- Warm orange/red hazard language so obstacles do not blend into the background.
- Reworked runner character with office jacket, shirt/tie, ID badge, face, hair, shoes, motion pose and energy effects.
- Reworked chair, meeting calendar, laptop crash, coffee spill, flying buzzword and urgent-task crate.
- Stronger floor/lane separation and contact shadows.
- Premium HUD/menu styling to match the game-world art direction.
