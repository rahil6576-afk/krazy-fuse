Social Sign-In Setup

Provider Disabled in Supabase

Facebook & Instagram sign-in require enabling the provider in your Supabase project dashboard.

💡
Why this happens:
Your Supabase backend ((from .env)) has not turned on the Facebook/Meta OAuth provider yet. Instagram authentication on the web uses Meta/Facebook Login.
⚙️ Quick Setup (Takes 2 minutes):
  1. Open your Supabase Dashboard: Authentication → Providers → Facebook.
  2. Toggle "Enable Facebook provider" to ON.
  3. Enter your Meta Client ID (App ID) and Client Secret from Meta for Developers.
  4. In your Meta App Settings, set the Valid OAuth Redirect URI to:
(https://<project>.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback)
